Cage

Shown when the trap config below has a cage. Once an animal is shut in the cage, choose what to do with it. If nothing is chosen it is released after cage_hold_hours. These don't put the trap into manual mode, but the buttons above do, and that ends the cage sequence. Kill and Killed are hidden for a cage with 1 door.

Once the animal has been released or let through to the kill mechanism, the trap waits up to cage_exit_wait_hours for it to leave the cage.

History

Messages sent to and received from the trap, newest first. A response has the same ID as the message it answers. "Lost" is a line that was garbled on the way from the trap, or an entry in the saved history that couldn't be read. File uploads only show the chunks that failed, and while the trap isn't answering only the first unanswered ping is shown. The camera keeps the last 2000 to 3000.

Trap Settings

Settings that don't apply to the chosen program or cage are hidden, and keep their saved values. Empty number fields use the trap's default, shown in grey. Saving restarts trap comms, and the trap too if its config changed.
